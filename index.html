Our Mission

Transforming knowledge into education, research, and social value.

The High Doctor Foundation is an independent non-profit organization dedicated to research, scientific dissemination, and cultural development. Born from years of experience in promoting education and responsibility, our mission is to build a healthier, more conscious, and connected society.

  • ✔ Disseminate knowledge based on scientific evidence.
  • ✔ Promote responsible use and harm reduction.
  • ✔ Facilitate collaboration between researchers, doctors, patients, and organizations.
  • ✔ Support research projects on medical cannabis.
  • ✔ Create accessible educational resources for society.
  • ✔ Combat misinformation related to cannabis.

Vision

To become a recognized European platform that connects people through culture, education, awareness and innovation. Building one of Europe's leading cannabis knowledge networks.
