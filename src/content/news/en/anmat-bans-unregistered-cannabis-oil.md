On 12 November 2026, Argentina's medicines regulator (ANMAT) published **Disposition 7206/2026** in the Official Gazette. It bans the use, sale (online included) and distribution of "Canna Oil" products nationwide until they obtain health authorisation.

## What happened

- The investigation began with a **complaint about an advertising leaflet** sent to the regulator.
- The leaflet and the product's website promoted it for a very long list of conditions: chronic pain, fibromyalgia, cancer, epilepsy, HIV, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, autism, insomnia, anxiety, depression, diabetes and more.
- ANMAT found the product **was not in the medicines register** and the linked establishments ("Canna Oil" and "Pura Sanación") **were not licensed**. Its composition and how it was made are unknown.

Infobae and La Nación, among others, covered it the same day; both agree on the disposition number, the reasons and the establishments.

## Why it matters beyond Argentina

It is a clear example of a myth we see often: **"any CBD oil will do"**. It will not.

- The only cannabidiol-only (THC-free) medicine authorised across the European Union (Epidyolex) has a **controlled composition**, a set dose and specific indications in rare, severe epilepsies, prescribed by a specialist.
- An unregistered oil may contain **more or less CBD than the label says**, undeclared THC or contaminants, and nobody has checked.
- **Be wary of any product that claims to help many unrelated diseases at once**: no real medicine works that way.

## Four things to check

1. **Does it have a health registration number?** Look it up on your country's regulator website (in Spain, AEMPS; in Argentina, ANMAT).
2. **Does it state the exact composition** (milligrams of CBD and THC) **and an independent lab analysis**?
3. **Does it promise to cure or treat serious diseases?** A product that is not a medicine cannot be advertised that way.
4. **Has a health professional who knows your treatment recommended it?** CBD interacts with other medicines.

This story is education, not medical advice. If you take an oil for a medical condition, talk to your doctor before changing anything.
